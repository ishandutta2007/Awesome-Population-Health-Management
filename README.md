Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects
Focused on Population Health Analytics, Care Management, Risk Stratification, Care Gaps, Value-Based Care & Clinical Data Intelligence
Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS platforms and open-source projects for Population Health Management (PHM). These tools help healthcare organizations aggregate clinical, claims, pharmacy, laboratory, social determinants of health (SDOH), and patient-generated data to identify high-risk populations, stratify patients, close care gaps, coordinate care, measure quality, and improve outcomes while controlling costs.

Examples include Innovaccer, Arcadia, Health Catalyst, ZeOmega, Lightbeam Health, Azara DRVS, Innovaccer InCare, Epic Healthy Planet, eClinicalWorks healow, and Oracle Population Health (the category leaders).

Open-source emphasis: This section is heavily expanded with major active projects for self-hosted clinical data platforms, population analytics, cohort discovery, care management, interoperability, FHIR servers, OMOP/OHDSI analytics, registries, dashboards, data pipelines, and public-health systems. Unlike the commercial market, relatively few open-source projects provide a complete end-to-end replacement for enterprise Population Health Management suites. However, the open-source ecosystem is exceptionally strong in the underlying components required to build one. OHDSI tools such as ATLAS, for example, provide open-source cohort-definition and observational-data analytics on standardized OMOP data.

Modern PHM platforms increasingly combine longitudinal patient records, risk stratification, quality measurement, care coordination, AI-assisted prioritization, and data intelligence. Oracle Health, for example, describes population-health capabilities spanning data aggregation, care coordination, quality management, analytics, and identification of care gaps and patient risks.

Open-Source GitHub Projects
Population Health, Cohort Discovery & Observational Analytics

OHDSI ATLAS
One of the strongest open-source foundations for population health analytics. ATLAS supports cohort definition, concept searching, patient profiles, population-level analyses, and observational research using data standardized to the OMOP Common Data Model.

OHDSI WebAPI
Open-source backend platform supporting ATLAS and other OHDSI applications for cohort definition, vocabulary access, analysis execution, and standardized observational healthcare data workflows.

OMOP Common Data Model
Open-source common data model for standardizing observational healthcare data across clinical, claims, pharmacy, and other sources.

OHDSI Achilles
Open-source data-characterization and quality-assessment tool for OMOP Common Data Model databases.

OHDSI ATLAS Characterization Tools
Open-source analytical tools for cohort characterization, population analysis, observational research, incidence measurement, and healthcare data standardization.

OHDSI CohortMethod
Open-source framework for population-level observational studies using cohort-based comparative analysis.

OHDSI PatientLevelPrediction
Open-source framework for developing and validating patient-level prediction models using standardized observational healthcare data.

OHDSI FeatureExtraction
Open-source tooling for extracting standardized patient features and covariates from OMOP data for analytics and predictive modeling.

OHDSI DataQualityDashboard
Open-source data-quality assessment framework for evaluating conformance, completeness, and plausibility of healthcare datasets.

OHDSI HADES
Open-source ecosystem of healthcare analytics tools supporting observational research, patient-level prediction, cohort analysis, evidence generation, and population analytics.

Open-Source Electronic Health Record and Clinical Platforms

OpenMRS
Major open-source electronic medical record platform designed for flexible healthcare environments and extensible clinical workflows. It provides a strong foundation for patient registries, longitudinal records, and population-health extensions.

OpenEMR
Free and open-source electronic health record and practice-management platform supporting clinical records, scheduling, billing, interoperability, and FHIR-related capabilities.

GNU Health
Open-source health and hospital information system supporting electronic health records, public health, epidemiology, patient management, and healthcare analytics.

Bahmni
Open-source hospital and healthcare information system built around OpenMRS and other open components, supporting clinical care, laboratory workflows, pharmacy, and healthcare operations.

LibreHealth EHR
Open-source healthcare software ecosystem supporting electronic health records and related clinical applications.

OpenClinic GA
Open-source healthcare and hospital information system supporting patient records and clinical administration.

OSCAR EMR
Open-source electronic medical record platform with a long history of use in primary-care environments.

OpenHospital
Open-source hospital information system supporting patient records, hospital workflows, clinical services, and healthcare operations.

Interoperability, FHIR & Health Data Exchange

HAPI FHIR
Open-source Java implementation of the HL7 FHIR interoperability standard and one of the most important building blocks for creating interoperable healthcare data platforms.

HAPI FHIR JPA Server Starter
Open-source starter implementation for deploying self-hosted FHIR servers with APIs, subscriptions, clinical terminology, and healthcare interoperability capabilities.

Firely Server
Open-source FHIR server ecosystem for building interoperable clinical data services and healthcare applications.

OpenMRS FHIR Module
Open-source interoperability module supporting FHIR-based exchange of healthcare information within the OpenMRS ecosystem. OpenMRS has explicitly adopted FHIR interoperability as part of its healthcare-system integration efforts.

Aidbox Community Tools
Open-source components and development tools related to FHIR-based healthcare application development.

Medplum
Open-source healthcare developer platform providing FHIR-native APIs, authentication, data storage, workflows, and application-building infrastructure.

OpenHIE
Open-source health information exchange architecture and ecosystem for interoperable healthcare systems, national health infrastructure, registries, and shared services.

OpenHIM
Open-source interoperability middleware for routing, monitoring, securing, and orchestrating health-information exchange between healthcare systems.

OpenFn
Open-source integration platform useful for connecting healthcare systems, registries, FHIR services, claims systems, and population-health data pipelines.

OpenSRP
Open-source digital health platform supporting longitudinal care, frontline health-worker workflows, population registries, and community health programs.

CommCare

SORMAS

OpenELIS Global
Open-source laboratory information system supporting public-health and clinical laboratory workflows.

OpenCRVS
Open-source civil registration and vital-statistics platform that can support population-level demographic and health-information ecosystems.

OpenLMIS
Open-source logistics-management platform supporting healthcare commodity and medicine supply chains, which can complement population-health programs.

iHRIS
Open-source human-resources information system designed for health-workforce management and planning.

Additional Strong Open-Source Options

Population analytics & cohort discovery: OHDSI ATLAS, WebAPI, HADES, CohortMethod, PatientLevelPrediction, Achilles, FeatureExtraction, and OMOP Common Data Model.

Electronic health records: OpenMRS, OpenEMR, GNU Health, Bahmni, LibreHealth, OSCAR EMR, and OpenHospital.

FHIR and interoperability: HAPI FHIR, Firely Server, Medplum, OpenMRS FHIR, OpenHIE, OpenHIM, OpenFn, and terminology-management systems.

Public health & registries: DHIS2, OpenSRP, CommCare, SORMAS, OpenCRVS, OpenELIS Global, OpenLMIS, and iHRIS.

Clinical quality & care-gap rules: Clinical Quality Framework, CQF Ruler, CQL tooling, OpenCDS, Drools, and FHIR-based decision-support frameworks.

Healthcare data integration: Apache NiFi, Apache Airflow, Airbyte, Meltano, dbt Core, Apache Kafka, Apache Spark, Apache Flink, Dagster, and Prefect.

Population-health dashboards: Apache Superset, Metabase, Grafana, Redash, Evidence, Apache Zeppelin, and custom React-based analytics portals.

Risk stratification & machine learning: PatientLevelPrediction, scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Jupyter, MLflow, and OMOP-standardized datasets.

Terminology and vocabulary services: Open Concept Lab, OHDSI vocabularies, SNOMED-compatible terminology tooling, FHIR terminology services, and custom terminology servers.

Patient registries: OpenMRS, DHIS2 Tracker, OpenSRP, CommCare, PostgreSQL-based custom registries, and FHIR Patient/Condition/Observation resources.

Care coordination: OpenMRS-based workflows, Medplum applications, OpenCDS, CQF Ruler, Drools, task-management systems, and custom FHIR workflow applications.

Many additional open-source projects exist for clinical registries, disease surveillance, FHIR applications, health information exchange, quality measurement, epidemiology, risk prediction, and healthcare data analytics.

Frameworks for building custom systems: Combine OpenMRS, OpenEMR, or GNU Health as the clinical-record foundation; use HAPI FHIR, Firely Server, or Medplum for interoperable healthcare APIs; standardize analytical data using the OMOP Common Data Model; use OHDSI ATLAS and HADES for cohort discovery and population analytics; and orchestrate data pipelines using Apache NiFi, Airflow, Dagster, or Prefect.

For analytics, store curated healthcare data in PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, or an OMOP-compatible warehouse, and build dashboards with Apache Superset, Metabase, or Grafana. Add CQL/CQF Ruler, OpenCDS, or Drools for care-gap identification and clinical rules, and integrate DHIS2 or OpenSRP where public-health registries and community-health workflows are required.

The strongest open-source architecture for a self-hosted Population Health Management platform is therefore generally a composable ecosystem rather than a single product: FHIR + OMOP + OHDSI + OpenMRS/OpenEMR + workflow orchestration + dashboards + clinical rules. OHDSI's open-source toolset is specifically designed to support standardized analytics on observational patient-level data and is particularly relevant for cohort and population analysis.

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Population Health Management systems process highly sensitive healthcare information and should be deployed with appropriate privacy, security, access-control, audit, consent, and data-governance safeguards.

Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction and may include HIPAA, GDPR, national health-data laws, clinical governance requirements, and healthcare interoperability standards.

Open-source projects listed here are not necessarily complete replacements for commercial enterprise PHM platforms; many are foundational components that require integration and customization.

Clinical risk scores, predictive models, care-gap recommendations, and automated prioritization require appropriate clinical validation and governance.

Data quality, terminology mapping, patient identity resolution, interoperability, and completeness of clinical and claims data can materially affect population-health analytics.

Self-hosted systems require ongoing maintenance, security patching, backups, interoperability testing, terminology updates, and operational support.

Population-health analytics and AI should support—not replace—qualified clinical judgment.
